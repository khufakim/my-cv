03 · Playbook

What to prepare before a portal opens.

You cannot submit a scholarship for someone else — signatures, transcripts and essays are yours. What you can do is arrive at every portal with the whole kit ready.

00Lock the facts that filter programmesAge, CGPA, degree status, restart decision
  • Exact age on each deadline. Türkiye requires under 21 for bachelor's; CSC and GKS under 25; MEXT 17–24; MEXT Research under 35; GKS Master's under 40.
  • CGPA and percentage. Most programmes want 60–80%+ or 2.5–3.0/4.0. Put the number in the profile bar above.
  • Degree status letter. You are in the 5th semester of BSSE, expected 2028. For master's applications you need a predicted-degree letter from your university.
  • Restart vs master's. A 2027 bachelor's abroad means starting from year one; transfer credit is rare for scholarship holders. Decide before writing essays.
  • Passport valid for 18+ months. Renew now if it expires before mid-2028.
01Documents vaultOne folder, PDF, named consistently
  • Passport scan + white-background photo (35×45 mm)
  • HSSC / A-Level transcript and certificate (attested)
  • Current university transcript + enrollment letter
  • IELTS / TOEFL score report (TRF number, valid 2 years)
  • CV — use the 2-page PDF already built
  • Master SOP + a shorter study plan
  • 2–3 recommendation letters, signed and dated
  • Medical form / HIV test (some portals ask after shortlisting)

02Essays and recommendersOne master SOP, then modular cuts
  • Draft the 700-word SOP: origin → why software engineering → evidence → why this country/programme → plan → why funding matters.
  • Cut a 1-page study plan for CSC and Türkiye (programme choices, timeline, return plan).
  • Write the leadership/community essay for Fulbright and Chevening.
  • Ask 2 professors + 1 supervisor at least 3 weeks before the first deadline; send them the CV and bullet points.
  • Get one outside reader to check grammar and consistency of dates.
SOP skeleton (fill the brackets)
Structure
Paragraph 1 — Hook (80 words)
Open with the concrete problem you want to solve with software. No childhood story unless it is specific.

Paragraph 2 — Evidence (150 words)
Two projects: what you built, the stack, the measurable result. Link the portfolio.

Paragraph 3 — Why this programme (150 words)
Name 2 modules and 1 professor/lab. Explain what you cannot learn in Pakistan.

Paragraph 4 — Why this country (100 words)
Be specific about the ecosystem, not the weather.

Paragraph 5 — Plan after graduation (100 words)
Job, research or venture; how you will transfer the knowledge back.

Paragraph 6 — Why funding (100 words)
State the need plainly. Tie it to the plan, not to sympathy.

03Portal sequenceWhich form opens when
  • HEC + CSC (open now): create the HEC account, complete the form, then the CSC form with Agency 5861. Both are mandatory.
  • KAIST (open now): application + recommendation letter by 21 Jan 2027, 18:00 KST.
  • Türkiye Bursları (10 Jan): tbbs account, programme choices, statement, interview prep.
  • Stipendium Hungaricum: watch the call page; prepare the Tempus account before it opens.
  • Fulbright: USEFP account opens ~Feb 2027; request GRE only if the call requires it.
04After you submitTrack, interview, visa
  • Save every application ID and confirmation PDF in the same folder.
  • Prepare a 90-second "tell me about yourself" and 3 project stories for interviews (Türkiye interviews run Apr–Jun).
  • Email the programme once if you have no confirmation within 10 days. Keep it short.
  • Start the visa and medical checks only after the award letter; do not pay agents upfront.

  • Restart or master's only — a 2027 bachelor's abroad means starting over; a 2028 master's is the lower-risk path.

04 · Alerts

A weekly email you actually own.

No passwords shared. The mailer runs inside your own Google account and reads the tracker data. Set it once; it runs every Monday at 09:00 Pakistan time.

Set up the Gmail digest

About 5 minutes. You approve Google's permission screen yourself.

  1. Open script.google.com → New project. Sign in as khuzaifakhufa@gmail.com.
  2. Delete the default myFunction and paste the script from the box on the right.
  3. Click Save, then run sendTestEmail. Approve access: Advanced → Go to project → Allow.
  4. Check your inbox for the test. Then run createWeeklyTrigger once.
  5. In Project Settings, set the time zone to Asia/Karachi so 09:00 means 09:00.
  6. Already published: this folder is live at khufakim.github.io/my-cv/scholarships and JSON_URL points there, so every upload refreshes the email.
